Analyst's reconciliation checks

  1. Fix the scope. Specify jurisdiction, office, election cycle and cutoff date before ranking donors.
  2. Identify the filer. Match legal committee name and ID, not just a candidate's name; distinguish party, PAC and authorized committee.
  3. Pair both sides. For a claimed transfer, compare the sender's expenditure with the recipient's receipt and cite both records.
  4. Separate mechanisms. Contributions, independent expenditures, loans, refunds and vendor payments have different meanings.
  5. Mark uncertainty. Record amendments, missing reports, unitemized amounts, duplicates and name-matching ambiguity.
  6. Compare conduct carefully. Cite the exact bill, roll-call number, chamber and date. Executive officials and challengers may have no legislative roll calls.

State filings cover statewide and legislative races; many local filings are held by county boards. Federal House records are at the FEC. No numeric funding totals are presented as reconciled facts here.
